# Analysis

## Layer 4, Head 11

I realized that on this head of the second layer, the head learned to pay attention between the "[MASK]" token and an adverb, and the adverb pays attention to the verb.

Example Sentences:
- I drank [MASK] in the morning
- Last night he ate [MASK] for dinner

## Layer 10, Head 10

On this layer, the model maintained the behavior of Layer 4, Head 11.

Example Sentences:
- I ran quickly to the [MASK] in the afternoon
- He did the [MASK] last night
